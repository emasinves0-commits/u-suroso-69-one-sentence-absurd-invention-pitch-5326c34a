## The pitch

> A doorbell that greets visitors in the voice of a GTA 6 NPC, trapping anyone who came to spoil the ending in an endless loop of the same three lines about traffic.

A doorbell weaponised as spoiler defence: anyone who shows up to ruin the ending of GTA 6 gets stuck in the NPC's endless small-talk loop — the same three lines about traffic, over and over — and never gets a word in edgeways. It is gloriously over-engineered for a problem nobody asked to solve, and instantly recognisable to anyone who has ever been cornered by an open-world NPC repeating the same line forever.
